# Follow, extend and send back (2026-11-17)

The finding is the research row bringup-home-follow-and-rhizoid. Three levels of device extension: data ([boot] and its files), hooks (executables named by the manifest), logic (a rust-script or small crate on the pinned bringup library). Pin by version line; send back by pull request plus an upstream item; pull by pin bump. Rhizoid backlog items filed: cutting-as-cargo-dependency, followers-one-pin-bump, send-back-writes-the-upstream-item (git-rhizoid.github.io PR 28).
